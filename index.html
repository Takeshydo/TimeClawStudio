About us

We are a small independent video game studio based in Lyon.
At TimeClaw, we want to convey the passion, creativity, and risk involved in making a studio succeed.
TimeClaw is also a studio that supports and co-develops with other studios to push each other to new heights and challenge the industry giants.

Time always presses down on us, but that is precisely what drives us to take risks and keep pushing boundaries.
